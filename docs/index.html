Our solution

Three things Bower gives back.

A fair go for small business in the AI economy.

🏠

Own the AI

Bower Box runs on the shop's own laptop or a small office computer: Inbox → Quote → Invoice → Compliance, fully offline.

🔒

Own the data

A federated co-op: shops answer each other's price questions from their own machines. There is no central database; only regional ranges leave.

📈

Own the upside

When your past jobs help another shop's accepted quote, you earn a per-use licence royalty, traced line by line.

The co-op rules

Benchmarks, never price-setting.

The rules keep price data historical, aggregated and anonymous, and they are enforced in code.

RuleWhy
≥ 5 different businesses behind every rangeNo single shop can be identified
Only jobs at least 90 days oldHistorical data, never live pricing
No business above 25% of the weightNo one dominates the benchmark
Regional P25–P75 ranges, never a recommended priceReference, not coordination
Same ranges visible to customersFair for households as well as tradies
Not enough data → refuseSilence beats a leak

Sovereignty by design

  • Open-weight models run locally through Ollama, with cloud features disabled
  • No offshore AI APIs; a live egress monitor shows public-internet connections = 0
  • Customer details are scrubbed on the contributor's own device before anything is shared
  • Untick or unplug to stop sharing, instantly
